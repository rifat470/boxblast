BoxBlast Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

BoxBlast Privacy Commitment: We never collect any personal data. This is a Telegram-based game where Telegram automatically sets your in-game name. We never ask for any private information. Just play regularly and collect points and gems. The more you collect, the more benefits and exciting surprises await you in future updates!

1. Data we collect

  • Telegram profile identification: Telegram automatically sets your public Telegram User ID and first name to identify your session. We never ask for or access your phone number, email address, contacts, private chats, or financial details.
  • Game data: coins, total earned, level, upgrades, selected box design, energy, daily-reward streak, task progress, invite relations (who invited whom), counters of ads watched per day, and timestamps of activity.
  • Technical data: standard secure server logs kept for security and debugging.

2. Why we use it

  • To create your account and keep your progress, balance and level safe, so you can always get them back.
  • To run features: leaderboard (shows first name, username and score), invites and referral rewards, daily rewards and tasks.
  • To prevent cheating and abuse and to keep the service reliable.
  • To show optional rewarded ads and to confirm that an ad was watched before a reward is given.

3. Account persistence

Your account is tied to your Telegram user ID. If you delete the App, log out of Telegram and come back, or open the App through another person’s invite link, you will get the same account and the same balance back. We do not create duplicate accounts for the same Telegram user.

4. Advertising

Ads are optional and start only when you tap a “Watch ad” button. To serve them the App uses Adsgram (adsgram.ai), Monetag and TADS. When you watch an ad, the respective network receives your Telegram user ID and may process technical data under its own privacy policy. We do not sell your personal data to advertisers.

5. Service providers

We use Supabase to store the database and Vercel to host the App and its API. These providers process data on our behalf. The App also relies on Telegram, which has its own privacy policy.

6. Sharing

We do not sell or rent your data. We share it only with the providers above, or when required by law.

7. Retention and deletion

We keep your game data for as long as your account is active, so that you can return at any time. You may ask us to delete your account and data by contacting us at the address below; we will do so within a reasonable time. Deleting only the Telegram chat or the App does not delete your data, because it is kept so that your balance can be restored.

8. Security

All requests are validated with Telegram’s signed login data, balances are calculated on the server, and the database is not directly accessible from the internet. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data.

9. In-game coins

Coins and points are virtual items for entertainment. They have no cash value and cannot be withdrawn, sold or exchanged for money.

10. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. Continued use means you accept the changes.
