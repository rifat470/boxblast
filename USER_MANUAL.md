# BoxBlast — Player Manual

## Quick start
1. Open the bot in Telegram and tap the app button. Your account is created automatically.
2. Open the **Blast** tab. Nine crates hide one box. Tap the crate you think hides it.
3. Earn coins, spend them in **Upgrades**, and level up to change the look of the whole app.

## Blasting
- You have **3 tries** per box. Find it on the first try for a bonus and a combo.
- A yellow **glint** hints where the box went, but not every glint is honest.
- Each blast uses **1 energy**. Energy recharges by itself, also while you are away.

## Upgrades
- **Boosts:** Blast power (more coins per blast), Energy tank (bigger energy), Recharge speed.
- **Warehouse:** Forklift, Conveyor, Scanner, Drone, Sorter, Depot, Hub, Air freight. Each level earns coins **every hour**, even with the app closed (up to 3 hours are collected at once, so check in a few times a day).

- Pick your box design in **Profile → Box designs**.

## Earn
- **Daily reward:** claim every day. Reward grows over a 7-day streak (miss a day and the streak restarts).
- **Tasks:** join the channel, invite friends, reach earning goals.
- **Optional ads:** tap **Watch ad** for bonus coins or an energy refill. Max 10 per day. The game works fully without ads.

## Friends
Friends tab → **Your personal invite link**. Share it. When a friend opens the app through your link, you get 2,500 coins and they start with 1,000.

## Your account is safe
Your account belongs to your Telegram profile. Deleting the app, logging out, or entering through someone else’s invite link never creates a new account and never resets your coins, levels or upgrades.

## Coins
Coins are in-game points with no cash value.
